Pinecone is the Minecraft launcher of the TwoGG community. Instances, mods and modpacks in one place, plus TwoGG events, event packs and your friends, right in the launcher.

Features

Everything you need to play

Pinecone takes care of versions, Java and downloads. You just decide what to play.

Instances

Every instance has its own game folder with mods, worlds and settings. Vanilla, Fabric, Quilt, Forge or NeoForge, any Minecraft version.

Discover

Browse modpacks, mods, resource packs, shaders and data packs and install them in one click, dependencies included.

Modpacks

Import from a file or link, updates with a backup first. Your own changes to configs and mods are kept.

.mrpackCurseForge ZIP

Change version

Move an instance to another Minecraft version or loader. Pinecone finds the matching mods.

Sync packs

Create your own collections like “Basics” or “Shader setup” once and switch them on and off in any instance.

Skins

Skin library with 3D preview and editor. Set your account's skin and cape right from the launcher.

Worlds & servers

Back up and tidy up worlds, see servers with live status and join directly. Screenshots and logs per instance.

Your look

Three themes (Bark, Pine, Winterland), five accent colors, pixel headings and reduced motion if you prefer.

Safe & open

Sign in with your Microsoft account, tokens in your system's keychain. Signed updates, source code open on GitHub.

TwoGG built in

Made for the community

Pinecone is not a launcher that just happens to support TwoGG. The community's events, servers and curated content are built right in.

Windows warning

The installer is not signed with a certificate yet. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info” and then “Run anyway”.

Running the AppImage

Make it executable after downloading: chmod +x Pinecone*.AppImage, then start it by double-clicking or from a terminal.

Found a bug?

In Pinecone, go to Settings → General → “Help & diagnostics”, create a diagnostics ZIP and report it with a short description.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Pinecone cost anything?

No. Pinecone is free and open source (MIT or Apache-2.0). You only need your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition.

Do I need a TwoGG account?

No. Your Microsoft account is enough to play. Connecting twogg.app is optional and shows your event registrations in the launcher. The friends list uses your Minecraft account.

Can I bring my modpacks from other launchers?

Yes. Modrinth packs (.mrpack) and CurseForge packs (ZIP) can be imported, as can Modrinth links. You can export your own instances as .mrpack and share them.

Are Forge and NeoForge supported?

Yes. Pinecone installs Forge and NeoForge with their official installers, including modpacks from Modrinth and CurseForge. Fabric and Quilt are supported too, of course.

How do I get updates?

Pinecone checks for new versions at startup and installs them when you agree. Updates are signed, and the launcher only installs packages from TwoGG.

Where is my data stored?

On Windows in %APPDATA%\TwoGG\Pinecone, on Linux in ~/.local/share/twogg-pinecone. Pinecone keeps sign-in data in your system's keychain, not in files.
